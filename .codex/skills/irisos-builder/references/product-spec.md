# IrisOS Product Spec

## Product Identity

IrisOS is an enterprise-grade SaaS operating system for modern agencies and creative enterprises. It unifies finance, operations, clients, projects, reporting, forecasting, intelligence, and content production from a single platform.

IrisOS includes Prisma AI as its proprietary intelligence layer. Prisma should appear across the product through names such as Prisma Insights, Prisma Forecasting, Prisma Recommendations, Prisma Reports, Prisma Analysis, Ask Prisma, and Prisma Command Center.

## Target Users

Build for freelancers, agencies, consulting firms, marketing organizations, creative studios, and multi-brand enterprises.

## Product Priorities

Generate every screen, workflow, dashboard, interaction, and realistic sample-data surface needed for a complete enterprise SaaS experience. Avoid placeholder pages and incomplete modules. Reuse enterprise components and maintain design consistency.

## Design Language

Use a premium, executive, enterprise-grade visual style inspired by Linear, Vercel, Stripe, Ramp, Salesforce, Monday Enterprise, Notion, and Mercury. Combine that SaaS clarity with a futuristic, sharp design language and metallic finishes.

Use primary colors from black, graphite, charcoal, titanium, gunmetal, metallic silver, and white. Use accent colors from brushed aluminum, platinum silver, and chrome highlights.

Do not use bright blue, bright green, bright red, neon colors, cartoon graphics, illustrations, or emojis.

## Design Hub

Design Hub is IrisOS's centralized platform for creative operations, content production, publishing, asset management, campaign design, creative analytics, and brand governance.

Canva powers the underlying design workflows while IrisOS remains the primary product.
