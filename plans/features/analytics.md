# Feature Plan: Analytics

## Goal

Build business intelligence dashboards that connect agency performance across finance, clients, projects, team, and creative operations.

## Screens

- Analytics overview
- Revenue analytics
- Client analytics
- Delivery analytics
- Creative analytics summary
- Cohort and trend analysis

## Build

- Show cross-module KPI trends, comparisons, cohorts, and benchmarks.
- Include configurable filters for date range, client, brand, service line, team, and channel.
- Add charts for revenue mix, margin trend, churn risk distribution, utilization, delivery health, and creative ROI.
- Surface Prisma analysis explaining major changes and anomalies.

## Data

Use aggregated seeded data from finance, clients, projects, team, Design Hub, and Prisma analysis records.

## Acceptance Checks

- Analytics data agrees with source modules.
- Filters affect charts and tables.
- Prisma explains at least three notable trends.
- Visual density remains readable on desktop and mobile.
