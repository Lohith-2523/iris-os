# Feature Plan: Team

## Goal

Build team operations for capacity forecasting, utilization, productivity insights, performance analysis, and hiring recommendations.

## Screens

- Team dashboard
- Capacity planner
- Utilization table
- Team member detail
- Hiring recommendations
- Performance insights

## Build

- Show utilization, available capacity, overloaded roles, hiring gaps, productivity trends, and project allocation.
- Add filters for role, team, brand, utilization band, and time period.
- Include Atlas capacity forecast, hiring recommendations, productivity insights, and performance analysis.
- Link team capacity to Projects and Forecasting.

## Data

Use users, roles, project allocations, utilization records, performance metrics, capacity forecasts, and Atlas team insights.

## Acceptance Checks

- Capacity calculations are deterministic and explainable.
- Overbooked team members are easy to identify.
- Hiring recommendations reference real workload data.
- Team member detail connects to projects and reports.
