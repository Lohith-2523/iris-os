# Agency Client Onboarding Framework

*A comprehensive onboarding structure for marketing, branding, creative, and growth agencies*

---

# Universal Client Information

**Required regardless of agency type**

These fields should be collected for every client before project kickoff.

---

## 1. Company Information

### Business Details

* Company Name
* Legal Entity Name
* Industry/Niche
* Business Description
* Website URL
* Headquarters Location
* Additional Operating Locations
* Company Size
* Years in Operation

### Business Model

* B2B / B2C / D2C / Marketplace / SaaS
* Primary Revenue Streams
* Product or Service Categories
* Average Customer Value

---

## 2. Primary Contacts

### Main Point of Contact

# Recommended Database Design (High-Level)

For a scalable agency platform:

```text
Organizations
├── Contacts
├── Goals
├── Competitors
├── Brand Profiles
├── Assets
├── Access Credentials
├── Projects
├── Services
├── Service-Specific Metadata
├── Invoices
├── Contracts
└── Reports
```

This structure is flexible enough to support a small social media agency, a full-service marketing agency, or a multi-division creative agency such as **Shards Media** while keeping onboarding standardized across all service lines.
